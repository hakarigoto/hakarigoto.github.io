Affiliate Disclosure

Plain-language version: some links on this site may pay us. Here is exactly how that works and what it does — and does not — change.

Last updated: August 2026

What an affiliate link is

Some pages on this site may contain affiliate links to third-party services. If you click such a link and then apply for, sign up for, or buy something from that company, this site may receive compensation from the company or its advertising network. You never pay more because of it.

How you can tell

  • Every page carries the notice you see at the top of the site.
  • Where an affiliate link or promotional module appears in a page, it is marked near the link itself — not just here on this page.
  • As of August 2026, the calculators under /en/ contain no affiliate links yet. This disclosure exists so that when they are added, the ground rules are already public.

What never changes

  • Calculators are neutral. The math in our tools does not know or care whether an affiliate relationship exists. Results are never adjusted to favor a paying service.
  • No pay-to-rank, no fake reviews. We do not rank services by what they pay us, and we do not write reviews of experiences we have not had.
  • Non-affiliate options stay on the page. Where free or do-it-yourself alternatives exist (like handling paperwork yourself), we keep them in the comparison.

Questions

If anything on this page seems inconsistent with what you see on the site, please open an issue on GitHub — we treat that as an error report.
